Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This is a sample privacy policy for a demo website. If you use this site as a starting point for a real business, have the policy checked for the country or countries you sell in.

Who we are

Davoris is a demo website for ride-on cars and motorcycles, built for portfolio purposes. Products are not for actual sale. You can reach us at destinyokeke394@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address and message. The form is handled by Formspree, which passes the message to us by email.
  • Newsletter sign-up: your email address. It is handled by Formspree and sent to us by email, and we use it only to send occasional emails about new rides and offers. You can ask us to remove it at any time.
  • Orders: when you tap "Order on WhatsApp", your cart, name and delivery address are placed in a WhatsApp message that you send yourself. We do not receive this information unless you send the message.

We do not run advertising, and we do not use analytics or tracking cookies.

What is stored in your browser

The site saves a few small items in your browser's storage on your own device:

NameWhat it isWhen it is used
davorisCart The rides in your cart and their quantities Always. It is needed for the cart to work.
davorisCookieConsent Your choice in the cookie notice Always. It stops the notice from asking again.
davorisCustomer The name and delivery address you type in the cart Only if you choose "Accept". It saves you typing them again.

Other services this site uses

  • Formspree receives what you type in the contact form and the newsletter box.
  • OpenStreetMap provides the map on the home page. It loads when you view that section.
  • WhatsApp opens when you tap a WhatsApp button. Its own privacy policy applies to what you send there.
  • Google Maps opens if you tap "Get Directions".

Your choices

  • Use the Cookie Settings link at the bottom of any page to change your choice. Choosing "Essential only" deletes your saved name and address.
  • Use "Clear cart" in the cart to empty it, or clear this site's data in your browser settings at any time.
  • To ask what messages we hold from you, or to have them deleted, email destinyokeke394@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the site handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
