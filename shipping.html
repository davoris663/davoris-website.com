Delivery times start from the day we dispatch your order, not the day you order. Delivery fees depend on your location and are confirmed on WhatsApp before you pay. Delivery is free on orders over $200 within Nigeria.

How your order is processed

  • You send your order on WhatsApp, and we reply to confirm availability, the total and the delivery fee.
  • Your order is reserved once payment is confirmed.
  • We dispatch within 1 to 2 business days and send you the courier's tracking details on WhatsApp.

Assembly and first use

Rides arrive partly assembled. Simple assembly instructions and the tools you need are in the box. Please read the manual and charge the battery fully before the first ride.

Check your order when it arrives

Please check the parcel when it is delivered. If anything is damaged or missing, tell us within 48 hours and send photos or a short video on WhatsApp so we can put it right quickly.

Returns

  • You can request a return within 7 days of delivery if the ride is unused and in its original packaging.
  • Message us on WhatsApp with your order details to start a return. Please do not send anything back before we confirm.
  • Return shipping is paid by the customer, unless the item is faulty or was not what you ordered.
  • Once we have received and checked the item, we refund you within 7 business days, using the way you paid.

Faulty or damaged items

If a ride arrives faulty or damaged, we will replace it or refund you, whichever you prefer, and we cover the return shipping.

Warranty

  • 6 months on the motor and electronics.
  • 3 months on the battery.
  • The warranty does not cover damage from misuse, riders above the weight limit, water damage, or normal wear on tyres.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel for free any time before we dispatch your order. Message us on WhatsApp as soon as you can.

Questions
