About Davoris

Davoris brings premium ride-on cars and motorcycles to kids who love adventure.

We started Davoris with one simple goal: giving kids the thrill of driving in style, without compromising on quality or safety. Every ride we sell is handpicked, checked, and built to bring smiles to young faces and peace of mind to parents.

From sleek sports cars to powerful ride-on motorcycles, our collection is designed to match every child's personality and sense of adventure. Whether it's a first ride or an upgrade, we make sure every product meets our standard before it reaches you.

As Davoris grows, we're committed to expanding our collection, improving our service, and making premium rides accessible to families across Nigeria and beyond.

About this demo

Davoris is a portfolio project that shows what a small online shop can look like. It is designed to work well on phones and includes:

  • A product collection with search, category filters, and sorting by price
  • A shopping cart that sends the order to WhatsApp
  • An FAQ page and a contact form that sends messages by email
  • Customer reviews and a store map
